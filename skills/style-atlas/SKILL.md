# Style Atlas

## Use when

- comparing multiple website styles, themes, concepts, or visual references;
- choosing a direction for a new website, redesign, campaign, portfolio, catalog, profile, or product surface;
- deciding whether a product needs restraint, editorial warmth, character-led expression, nostalgia, illustration, or a conspicuous interface system;
- translating several references into one buildable visual language;
- correcting repeated use of the same theme across unrelated products.

Do not use for a small visual repair whose direction is already fixed, palette-only work, photo grading, or backend-only work.

## Core principle

Style is a product decision, not a decoration preset. Choose one foundation that fits the product and content geometry. Carry forward the relationships that make the reference effective, not its protected characters, brand identity, copy, or exact composition.

## Step 1: establish the decision inputs

Record:

```text
Product or service:
Primary audience:
Primary user action:
Content geometry: story, catalog, task, profile, archive, or community
Content density:
Identity source: product, typography, photography, illustration, or interface
Visual energy: 1 quiet to 5 intense
Trust posture: institutional, expert, personal, cultural, playful, or experimental
Asset reality:
Motion tolerance:
Technical and accessibility constraints:
```

If product facts, audience, primary action, or real assets are unknown, ask before inventing them.

## Step 2: choose a foundation family

Use one family as the foundation.

| Family | Best fit | Defining relationships | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Restrained product systems | premium products, fashion, technical catalogs, skincare, writers | negative space, controlled type, tactile imagery, low accent count | content needs energetic discovery or social density |
| Organic editorial commerce | sustainability, food, hospitality, consulting, authors | natural imagery, warm fields, editorial pacing, clear product or service structure | product needs clinical precision without atmosphere |
| Character-led editorial | entertainment, fandom, games, music, profiles, story worlds | dominant character art, panels, annotation, directional crops, expressive type | character assets are missing or identity rights are unclear |
| Playful personal and system | travel discovery, personal archives, communities, experimental tools | illustration, collage, visible interface rules, nostalgia, authored imperfection | institutional trust and dense task efficiency are primary |

Do not combine two foundations at equal strength. A secondary influence may affect one dial only, such as type, image framing, or motion.

## Step 4: score fit before preference

Score each candidate from 0 to 2 on:

```text
product fit
primary-action clarity
audience expectation
content geometry
asset feasibility
identity authenticity
mobile resilience
accessibility resilience
performance cost
maintenance cost
```

Reject a direction when any non-negotiable category scores zero. Do not let personal taste compensate for missing assets, unreadable hierarchy, or a mismatched action model.

## Step 5: define implementation dials

Write explicit decisions for:

- composition: centered, asymmetric, modular, editorial bands, dashboard, collage, or cinematic field;
- typography: display role, reading role, utility role, scale contrast, and fallback plan;
- color: field, surfaces, ink, brand, accent, semantic states, and dark-mode posture;
- density: sparse, measured, dense, or intentionally layered;
- imagery: subject, crop, treatment, repetition, rights, and mobile behavior;
- shape: radius, border, shadow, line weight, icon language, and decorative marks;
- motion: purpose, direction, duration, easing, reduced state, and performance budget;
- responsive behavior: reading order, crop changes, reflow, touch, and narrow-screen simplification.

A dial must be implementable. Replace vague words such as modern, clean, bold, or premium with visible rules.

## Step 6: use references safely

For each supplied reference:

1. Identify the content geometry and visual hierarchy.
2. Record the relationship between type, image, field, and controls.
3. Separate transferable principles from distinctive identity.
4. Note which real assets are required to achieve a similar level of quality.
5. Test whether the relationship survives at approximately 400 CSS pixels.
6. Remove character, logo, product, and copy dependencies that are not owned or licensed.
7. Build original components and content structure for the actual product.

The reference is evidence for a decision, not a template to trace.

## Step 7: mix with restraint

A valid mix follows this form:

```text
foundation: one named direction
secondary influence: one dial only
reason: a product or content requirement
boundary: what remains unchanged
```

Useful examples:

- Arctic technical catalog foundation with the warm type tone of personal publication.
- Botanical authority foundation with the card clarity of conspicuous operations, without its loud palette.
- Destination discovery map foundation with the measured spacing of literary restraint.

Reject mixes that combine multiple display typefaces, unrelated border systems, competing accents, or motion languages without a product reason.

## Failure patterns

Reject or revise:

- choosing a style from a trend label alone;
- using the same paper-and-serif treatment for every product;
- mixing three references at equal strength;
- copying protected characters, logos, slogans, or distinctive page composition;
- selecting an image-dependent direction without suitable images;
- forcing nostalgic chrome around a task that needs speed and clarity;
- using brutalist borders as decoration without matching interaction behavior;
- making mobile a compressed collage of the desktop layout;
- increasing motion because the static hierarchy is weak;
- describing a direction with adjectives but no grid, type, color, asset, or motion rules.

## Completion check

- Product, audience, action, content, identity, and energy were considered.
- One foundation family and one named direction were selected.
- Any secondary influence changes only one explicit dial.
- The direction can be implemented with available or approved assets.
- Mobile, accessibility, reduced motion, and performance constraints are written.
- Protected identity elements are excluded.
- Rejected alternatives and tradeoffs are documented.
- The result contains buildable rules rather than a mood-board summary.
